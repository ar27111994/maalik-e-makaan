## How the calculator works

The tax is worked out on the **gross rent for the year**: the rent plus any charge for furniture, fixtures or services connected with the property. A monthly rent is multiplied by 12.

For a landlord who is an individual or an association of persons (AOP), the [FBR rate card for tax year 2027](https://download1.fbr.gov.pk/Docs/202681113864992WithholdingTaxRatesCard2027.pdf) sets these bands on the gross annual rent:

| Gross rent for the year             | Landlord on the ATL                                 | Landlord not on the ATL                             |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| up to Rs 300,000                    | nil                                                 | nil                                                 |
| over Rs 300,000, up to Rs 600,000   | 5% of the amount over Rs 300,000                    | 10% of the amount over Rs 300,000                   |
| over Rs 600,000, up to Rs 2,000,000 | Rs 15,000 plus 10% of the amount over Rs 600,000    | Rs 30,000 plus 20% of the amount over Rs 600,000    |
| over Rs 2,000,000                   | Rs 155,000 plus 25% of the amount over Rs 2,000,000 | Rs 310,000 plus 50% of the amount over Rs 2,000,000 |

For a landlord that is a company, the rate is 15% of the gross rent if it is on the ATL and 30% if it is not, according to the [FBR's tax year 2026 card](https://download1.fbr.gov.pk/Docs/20258181281745641WHT-RateCard.pdf); the excerpt of the tax year 2027 card we could read did not show the company row, and neither the [FBR's circular on the Finance Act 2026](https://download1.fbr.gov.pk/Docs/202698139527880CircularNo.2of2026-27.pdf) nor the other sources we read mention a change.

The calculator works in exact figures and rounds only to show the result. Each band includes its upper edge: a rent of exactly Rs 600,000 is in the second band.

### Where the figures are less certain

- **Landlords not on the ATL.** The current FBR cards double the rates for an individual or AOP not on the ATL, under rule 1 of the Tenth Schedule. The FBR's 2020 and 2021 cards said that rule did not apply to section 155, and we have not found the law that changed this. Treat that column with care, and check it with a tax adviser.
- **Monthly payments.** The bands are set on a year's gross rent. We found no official rule for spreading the tax over monthly payments, a part year or a mid-year change of rent. The monthly figure the calculator shows is simply the year's tax divided by 12.

## Who has to deduct

Only a tenant who is a "prescribed person" has to deduct. The [FBR's guidance](https://download1.fbr.gov.pk/Docs/20186291463931170Brochure_Collectionanddeductionoftaxatsource.pdf) lists:

- the federal, provincial and local governments;
- companies;
- non-profit organisations and charitable institutions;
- the diplomatic missions of foreign states;
- private educational institutions, boutiques, beauty parlours, hospitals, clinics and maternity homes;
- individuals and associations of persons who pay gross rent of Rs 1.5 million or more in a year.

So a family renting a house for less than Rs 1.5 million a year does not deduct tax under section 155. We have not seen the 2026 text of the law itself, only this guidance.

## Check the ATL

To check whether a person is on the FBR's Active Taxpayers List, send a text message to 9966 with the word ATL, a space, and the 13-digit CNIC number (for a company or AOP, its 7-digit NTN instead), or search the [ATL on the FBR website](https://e.fbr.gov.pk/ATLSearchUtilityIT.aspx).

## What the deduction means for you

Tax deducted under section 155 is adjustable: it counts towards your final income tax for the year, which depends on all your income and allowances. Since tax year 2022, an individual's income from property has been taxed with the rest of their income, after deductions such as the one-fifth allowance for repairs. The [guide to tax on rental income](/guides/rental-income-tax/) explains more. Neither this calculator nor the Maalik-e-Makaan app estimates your final tax.
