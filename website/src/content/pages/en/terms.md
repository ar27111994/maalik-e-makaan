## This website

You may read, print and share the pages of this website. The guides and calculators are free to use.

### The guides and calculators are information, not advice

- The guides are general information about the law in Pakistan as we understood it on the date each one shows. The law changes, and its application depends on your circumstances. They are not legal or tax advice.
- Each guide cites the official sources it relies on. Where sources disagree or are unclear, the guide says so. Check the official source, or ask a lawyer or tax adviser, before you act.
- The calculators show their working, so you can check every figure. The tax calculator shows tax withheld under section 155 only. It does not work out your final tax.
- We try hard to be accurate, but we cannot promise that every page is complete, correct or up to date. If you find a mistake, please [tell us on GitHub](https://github.com/ar27111994/maalik-e-makaan/issues).
