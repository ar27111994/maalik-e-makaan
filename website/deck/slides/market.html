Our estimate: subscriptions alone are about US$20M a year, at Rs 100 per rented home a month. The larger prizes are overseas owners paying in foreign currency, property managers, and payments on a residential rent roll near US$4 billion a year.

Sources: PBS Census 2023, National Housing Policy 2025, SBP FY26 (via press reports). Estimates are ours.

Census 2023 counted about 38.3 million households; the National Housing Policy puts 11.87% in rented homes (about 4.55 million) and 23.82% of urban households (about 3.58 million). The policy also says no department holds complete records of rental units, which is our point: the records live with landlords. Remittances were US$41.6 billion in FY26. The estimate: 4.55 million rented homes at Rs 100 a month is Rs 5.45 billion, about US$19.7 million a year; at Rs 50 it halves, at Rs 200 it doubles. The rent roll estimate assumes Rs 20,000 a month per home. These market figures came from search summaries because the research session could not open PBS, SBP or the policy PDF; check them against the primary documents before sending.
