Roadmap

From a trusted ledger to trusted agents

Now to Feb 2027

Launch, then listen

  • Google Play and App Store
  • Native Urdu and legal review
  • Audit-trail screen
  • Read-only MCP server
  • “Speak or paste a payment”
  • Urdu amount parser and tests

6 to 12 months

The assistant

  • WhatsApp assistant, with voice
  • MCP writes within limits
  • Tenant portal and tenant agent
  • Reconciliation suggestions
  • Documents read on the phone
  • Hostel and PG mode

12 to 24 months

Money that moves

  • Raast Request-to-Pay, through a licensed partner
  • Open-banking data, once SBP’s rules are final
  • An oversight agent for owners abroad
  • Landlord, tenant and caretaker agents that coordinate

Plans, not commitments; each step ships only when the one before it is trusted.

Horizon one finishes the launch: store listings (Play is waiting on a D-U-N-S number), a native Urdu review and a legal review, and the audit-trail screen, which must exist before any agent can write. Then a read-only MCP server and in-app drafting from a voice note or pasted SMS, always confirmed, built on a tested parser for Urdu numerals, lakh and crore and Roman Urdu. Horizon two brings the WhatsApp assistant and the tenant side. Horizon three moves money: Raast Request-to-Pay needs a licensed payment partner, and SBP’s draft open-banking regulations were out for consultation in November 2026. The last item is agents that coordinate across roles, using the A2A protocol.
