Maalik-e-Makaan

مالکِ مکان

One rent ledger Pakistani landlords can trust, in English and Urdu. Built so every rupee is recorded once and exactly, whoever records it: a landlord, a caretaker or an AI agent.

Version {{version}}, released {{releaseDate}} · Android, iOS and web

Maalik-e-Makaan means “owner of the house”. It is a rent ledger for Pakistani landlords: who owes what and since when, receipts, deposits, caretakers, police registration deadlines and tax-year statements, in English and Urdu. The screen on the right is the real app with sample data. Version 1.0 shipped on 9 November 2026. The company is AR Labs (SMC-Private) Limited.
