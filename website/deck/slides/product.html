The product

One ledger for the whole tenancy

Ledger and receipts

Dues raise themselves. Cash, bank, Raast, JazzCash or Easypaisa, with numbered receipts in English or Urdu.

WhatsApp reminders

In English, Urdu or Roman Urdu, from the landlord’s own number, with a gentle suggestion to wait late at night or during Friday prayers.

Deposits

Held, applied at move-out and refunded, with a running balance for every tenancy.

Owners abroad

Four roles. A caretaker records cash and hands it over; the owner sees every rupee from abroad.

Paperwork deadlines

Police registration by province, agreement and e-stamp details, and CNICs masked on screen.

Tax-year statements

July–June statements, with tax withheld by company tenants under section 155.

Also in v1.0: expenses, utility bills and meter readings, maintenance, documents, a dashboard of what needs attention, and exports.

Everything here is in the released app (v1.0.1). Dues are generated each month with proration, late fees and rent increases; payments can be recorded as cash, bank transfer, Raast, JazzCash, Easypaisa, cheque or card; receipts are numbered without gaps and printable in English or Urdu. Reminders open WhatsApp with a ready message, so there is no per-message cost; between 10 pm and 8 am Pakistan time, during Friday prayers, or within three days of the last reminder, the app suggests waiting, and the landlord decides. Four roles: owner, manager, accountant, viewer. The app records payments; it does not process them yet.
