Global tools are priced in US dollars, in English or Spanish, built around US law and bank rails. Pakistani portals sell listings, not record-keeping. Agency and hostel software is aimed at businesses with staff. The simple ledger apps are free and thin: no deposits, no caretakers, no compliance. What only we combine: Urdu as an equal language, WhatsApp reminders in three languages, deposits and caretaker handovers, police registration deadlines and section 155 tax. Our honest gaps: we do not process payments yet, there is no tenant portal yet, and we are not yet on the Play Store or the App Store.
