The problem

Rent runs on registers, cash and WhatsApp threads

01

Who owes what, and since when?

Part payments, late fees and old arrears live in a paper register or a spreadsheet. Nobody can see how long each rupee has been owed.

02

Cash in a caretaker’s hand

An overseas owner’s relative collects the rent in cash and hands it on later. The owner abroad cannot see every rupee.

03

Paperwork with penalties

Police tenant registration within days, agreements and e-stamp, the Rent Registrar, and tax withheld by company tenants under section 155.

Deadlines and tax rules as researched for the website’s guides: police registration within 15 days in Punjab and 48 hours in Sindh.

Three pains we heard and researched. One: arrears. Rent is paid in parts and late, and the register cannot show how old a debt is. Two: caretakers. Many owners live abroad and a relative collects in cash. Three: paperwork. Police registration of tenants is required by law, with short deadlines and real enforcement; Punjab expects a written agreement with the Rent Registrar; company tenants withhold tax under section 155 of the Income Tax Ordinance. The app handles all three.
