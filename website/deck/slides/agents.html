Where it goes

Munshi: the assistant drafts, the landlord confirms

Speak or paste a payment

A voice note, a bank or wallet SMS, or a Raast screenshot becomes a draft payment. The landlord confirms, and the same idempotent function records it.

Bring your own AI

An MCP server lets the landlord’s own assistant read the ledger first, then make confirmed, additive changes.

Reminders and reconciliation

Drafts reminders in Urdu, Roman Urdu or English, and matches incoming Raast credits to open dues for a person to approve.

Tenants and documents

A tenant asks for their own balance and receipts; CNICs and agreements are read with masking and recorded consent.

Proposed. Munshi (منشی) is the clerk who once kept a landlord’s books.

This is the proposed agent layer, not yet built. The principle: the model drafts, a person confirms, and the database decides what is allowed. Payments drafted from a voice note or a pasted SMS go through the same record_payment function the app uses, with an idempotency key taken from the message, so a resent SMS cannot count twice. The MCP server starts read-only (who owes, statements, the overdue list) and later adds confirmed, additive writes; voids, waivers, deletions and team changes are never exposed to an agent. Urdu speech recognition is good enough for voice notes (published error rates around 3.5% of characters); Punjabi and Saraiki are not yet, so those fall back to tap-to-confirm.
