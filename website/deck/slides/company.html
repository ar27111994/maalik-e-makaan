AR Labs (SMC-Private) Limited is a single-member private company incorporated with the SECP on 20 October 2026 at the Islamabad Company Registration Office, CUIN 0361950. It is a product company: Maalik-e-Makaan is its product, and the code is public on GitHub under the MIT licence. The founder, Ahmed Rehan, is a full-stack engineer working since 2014: a front-end engineer for four years (2018 to 2022) on Eagle 6, an enterprise cybersecurity product, where he also helped hire and mentor the front-end team; more than 40 freelance jobs on Upwork with a 5.0 rating; and open-source work including a webhook debugger and an agent-tooling CLI. Master of Computer Science, Arid Agriculture University, Rawalpindi; BSc, University of the Punjab. Source: his résumé at ar27111994.dev.
