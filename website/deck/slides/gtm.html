Go to market

Start with the owners who feel it most

Owners abroad first

They pay in strong currencies and can’t see their caretaker’s cash. About 975,000 Roshan Digital Accounts by September 2026. Reach them through banks’ overseas desks and ads in Saudi Arabia, the UAE, the UK and the US.

Estate agents as partners

Agents already earn about a month’s rent for each letting. A free Business seat for their first landlords, and a referral fee on every paid plan.

Loops that cost nothing

Every WhatsApp receipt and reminder carries a link. Free guides and calculators on the website answer landlords’ questions in English and Urdu.

Roshan Digital Account count from press reports of SBP data.

Overseas owners are the highest-value segment: they earn in dirhams, riyals, pounds and dollars, they depend on a relative to collect cash, and they already use banking products built for them, such as Roshan Digital Accounts. Estate agents are a natural channel because they place the tenant in the first place. The website’s guides on police registration, rent increases and rental tax, and its three calculators, are free and in both languages; every receipt and reminder sent from the app carries a small link back. Facebook groups and housing societies are experiments we have no data on yet.
