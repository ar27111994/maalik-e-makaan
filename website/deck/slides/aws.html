Today’s ledger runs on Supabase (Postgres) and Vercel. The credits fund the new AI and messaging layer, at an estimated fraction of a US cent per assistant action.

The credits would go to the new agent layer, not today’s bills: Supabase and Vercel are billed outside AWS. Bedrock runs Claude for the assistant; our estimate is about half a US cent per action at list prices or less with the newest small models, and each plan would cap actions per month. Textract does not read Urdu, so Urdu documents go to Claude’s vision on Bedrock, with CNIC numbers masked before the call. Urdu support in Transcribe must be tested before we promise voice notes on AWS. We keep data in ap-south-1, next to the database, and will measure latency to me-central-1 from Pakistani networks before moving anything.
