Sources: SBP Payment Systems Reviews FY26; PTA 2025–26; PTA chairman, 3 Nov 2026 (via press reports).

The rails exist: Raast handled 742 million payments worth Rs 23.3 trillion in a single quarter. Digital payments are 92% of retail transactions by count but roughly 29 to 33% by value, so big-ticket payments such as rent are still largely cash; we found no published survey of how rent is paid, which is itself an opening. PTA reports about 200 million cellular subscriptions and that about 72% of devices on the networks are smartphones (a device share, not a share of people). The 100 million WhatsApp figure is accounts, not active users. Check each against the SBP and PTA documents before sending.
