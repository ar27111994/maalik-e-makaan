The agentic era

Property software is turning into agents.

AppFolio, Yardi, Entrata and EliseAI shipped AI agents in 2026, and AppFolio’s now takes actions from inside Claude. All of them serve large US landlords. None serves a cash-first, Urdu-speaking one.

Maalik-e-Makaan: the rent ledger an AI can be trusted with.

The pattern across the incumbents: they are opening their systems of record to outside agents, mostly through the Model Context Protocol, with the user’s permissions as the guard-rail. AppFolio’s Realm-X connector for Claude is listed in Claude’s connector directory, which we checked. The Yardi, Entrata and EliseAI launches came from press summaries; check them before sending. Our angle: the same shift, for a market none of them reach, on a ledger whose rules and proofs live in the database.
