Business model

Three ways to charge, to test with landlords

Option A

Plans by size

Free for up to 3 units.

Pro at Rs 499 a month, up to 25 units.

Business at Rs 2,500 a month, plus Rs 50 a unit above 25.

Option B

Free ledger, paid assistant

The ledger stays free and open source.

Munshi, the assistant, is a paid add-on with a monthly allowance of actions.

Option C

A plan for owners abroad

US$4.99 a month or $49 a year, paid in the owner’s own currency.

Caretaker accountability and a monthly statement.

In every option, later: revenue linked to payments on Raast, once rent can be collected in the app. Nothing is priced yet.

Anchors: Pakistani business software from about Rs 2,500 a month; property managers charge 5–10% of rent.

We have not chosen a model, so these are three options to test in landlord interviews. Option A anchors Pro just above a music subscription and Business at the entry price of Pakistani business software. Option B keeps the open-source ledger free and charges for the AI layer, where our costs are. Option C charges overseas owners in their own currency through the app stores. Plan on 1 to 2% of installs paying, which is what mobile apps see in South Asia, not the 3 to 5% of global software. The price anchors came from press and vendor pages we could not open in this session; check them before sending.
