# Data map

Part of the [legal review pack](README.md). This is not legal advice. It lists what Maalik-e-Makaan
stores about whom, where, who can see it, how long it stays, and which services handle it. It
separates what is **live now** (the app's version 1.1.1 and the website) from what is **planned**
under the owner's decisions 1 to 3, none of which is live.

- **RES:** `supabase/migrations/20261112092618_residence.sql` in pull request
  [#61](https://github.com/ar27111994/maalik-e-makaan/pull/61) at commit `6608f1e` (decision 1,
  built but not merged and not applied to the hosted project).

## Who is who

- **Account holder:** a person with an account: an owner, or someone an owner invited as manager,
  accountant or viewer (`public.member_role`, M-identity:18). The roles and what each may do are in
  [`docs/architecture/permissions.md`](../architecture/permissions.md).
- **Caretaker:** under decision 1, always an account holder with the manager role, named by an
  owner (PJ:102-103; RES:25-31).
- **Tenant:** has no account and no access in this version. A tenant sees only what the landlord
  gives them: receipts, statements and WhatsApp messages.
- **Other people:** a tenant's references, emergency contact and co-occupants, and anyone named on
  a letterhead.
- **AR LABS:** AR LABS (SMC-PRIVATE) LIMITED, which runs the website and the app (PEN:58).

In the tables, "members" means every account holder in the portfolio, and the roles are O (owner),
M (manager), A (accountant) and V (viewer). "PD" means "deleted with the portfolio", which happens
when an owner deletes it, or when the account of its only owner is deleted. "B90" means "then kept
up to 90 days more in the encrypted weekly backups".

## Gaps between the code, the live notice and the plans

Found while building this map. Each is for the owner, and where marked, for the lawyer.

1. **Tenant erasure.** A tenant with any tenancy cannot be deleted, only archived
   (M-leases:90-91), and there is no "erase this tenant's identifying data" action. The audit
   trail keeps a deleted tenant's or occupant's details, except the CNIC, until the portfolio is
   deleted (M-audit:294-353). The purge is planned for 2027 ([retention](../roadmap/retention.md)).
   Lawyer: [T2](questions.md#tenants).
2. **Every role can read full CNICs and open CNIC images** through the app's server; masking is a
   screen default (M-tenants:288-290; M-docs). Lawyer: [T3](questions.md#tenants).
3. **Teammates read each other's e-mail and phone** (M-identity:655-657). The live notice does not
   say so. Lawyer: [C7](questions.md#residence-and-caretakers).
4. **The reminder log keeps the whole message**, up to 1,000 characters with bank details. The live
   notice says so since #62 (`fabc088`, PEN:27); the retention plan still cuts the text down.
   Lawyer: [T4](questions.md#tenants), whether the app should keep less.
5. **Full tenant rows, CNICs included, are cached on the device unencrypted** until sign-out, in
   the browser's local storage on the web (row 14). Lawyer: [T7](questions.md#tenants).
6. **An accepted invitation keeps the invitee's e-mail** after that person deletes their account,
   until the portfolio is deleted (M-deletion:1475-1476 deletes only invitations never accepted).
   The notice the other owners got when the invitation was accepted keeps the e-mail for up to
   180 days (row 4).
7. **Uploaded PDFs keep their metadata**; only photos are cleaned
   ([`document_files.dart`](../../app/lib/src/features/documents/data/document_files.dart):134-170).
8. **The sign-up name stays in Supabase Auth's metadata** after the person changes their name
   (`auth_repository.dart`).
9. **No data export for account holders, and no route for tenants.** The CSV exports cover the
