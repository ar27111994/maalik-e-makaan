### In short

- This website sets no cookies. It counts visits with PostHog, in the United States, without knowing who you are, and you can switch that off on this page.
- The calculators work inside your browser. Nothing you type into them leaves your device.
- The app sends nothing to analytics or support chat until you switch them on in Settings, and you can switch them off at any time.
- In the app, your records are yours. Only the people you invite can see them, as far as their role allows, and the services that run the app for us handle them only to do that. They are never sold, and never used for advertising.
- The details you record about your tenants are yours to look after. We keep them for you and use them only to run the app.

### This website

The site is a set of plain pages. Its fonts, images and scripts are served from the site itself.

#### Counting visits

We count visits with [PostHog](https://posthog.com/privacy), so that we know which pages people read and which they leave. PostHog does this for us, and keeps the data in the United States (more under "Where it is kept").

- No cookie is set, and nothing is stored in your browser to count you.
- When you open a page, your browser sends the page's address to this site, which passes it on to PostHog. The site first removes anything after a "?" in the address. As with any request, your IP address and your browser's description (its "user agent") go with it.
- PostHog turns your IP address, your browser's description and this site's name, together with a random value that changes every day, into a code. It removes your IP address before it stores the visit, and deletes each day's random value once that day's visits are counted. So a visit today and a visit tomorrow look like two different visitors, and the code cannot be matched to you later. [Lawyer: A2] [To confirm: RM:316's preview check that PostHog receives the visitor's IP address through the site's rewrite. If it does not, PostHog counts page views but not visitors, and this bullet and the FAQ answer change.]
- No location is worked out from your IP address. Nothing records your screen, your clicks or what you type, and the site shows no surveys.
- PostHog keeps no profile of you, and we do not try to find out who you are. [To confirm: the website's configuration sets `person_profiles: 'never'` and never calls `identify` (changes table, row 9).]
- [Owner: list what each counted visit carries, from the final configuration: for example the page, the site that linked to it, and the type of browser and device.]

#### Switching counting off

- Use the switch on this page. Your browser then remembers your choice for this site, and the site stops loading the counter. That choice is the only thing this site keeps in your browser. [Lawyer: A3]
- If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal, the counter does not load at all. [Lawyer: A4]

#### Hosting

Like every website, its pages are delivered by a hosting provider, [Vercel](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy) [To confirm: in the United States], whose servers necessarily receive the technical details of each request, such as your IP address and the page asked for. The visit counts reach PostHog through Vercel. We do not use these details to identify you.

#### The calculators

The calculators work inside your browser. Nothing you type into them leaves your device, and the visit counts never include it.

### The app

This section describes the app from version [Owner: the version in which these changes ship], released on [Owner: date], and the versions after it.

#### What it stores

- **Your account:** your email address, your name, your phone number if you give one, and your choice of language. The sign-in service, Supabase Auth, also keeps a scrambled (hashed) form of your password and sign-in details such as when you last signed in, and the IP address and the browser or app each sign-in came from.
- **Where you live, if you tell us:** the country you live in and your time zone. You choose them yourself. The app may suggest the time zone your phone is set to, but saves only what you confirm. The country and time zone the app keeps for you are only what you tell it: we never work them out from your phone number, the country of your app store account, your IP address, your language or how you pay. We never ask for or store your nationality or immigration status.
- **Your portfolio's details:** its name; the name and phone number printed on its receipts; the Raast ID or bank account you choose to show tenants; and how receipts are numbered. Also the answer to "Does the owner of these properties live abroad?", which an owner or manager answers in the app, and who the portfolio's caretaker is, and since when.
- **Letterheads:** the letterhead a portfolio prints on its documents, its Urdu version if you add one, and whether receipts are printed on A5 or A4 paper. A letterhead is an image you upload (a PNG or JPG file) [Owner: or a PDF, once PDF uploads ship]. Or you make one in the app, from a logo and details such as a business name in English and Urdu, an address, and a phone number or email address. [Owner: the builder's fields are not decided.]
- **What you enter:** your properties and units; your tenants' details (such as names, CNIC numbers, phone numbers and references); tenancies, rent, payments, receipts, expenses, utility bills and meter readings; and any photos or documents you attach.
- **Reminders:** a log of each WhatsApp reminder you open from the app: when, to which tenancy, in which language, and the message itself, up to its first 1,000 characters. That message can include the tenant's name, the amount due and the payment details shown to the tenant. [Lawyer: T4]
- **Who did what:** an audit trail of changes to the records and the money, with who made each one and when, so that an owner can trust what a caretaker records. It also records who answered whether the owner lives abroad, who named the caretaker, and changes to letterheads. It keeps the earlier values of records that are changed or deleted. CNIC numbers are not written into that trail, and neither is where you live.
- **Notifications:** the messages in your inbox in the app, which can name a tenant, a unit and an amount.
- **Your privacy choices:** for each of the switches described under "Your privacy choices", whether it is on, the version of the text you saw, when you switched it on or off, and how.

#### Who can see it

- Only the people you invite to a portfolio can see its records, and each person only as far as their role allows: owner, manager, accountant or viewer. [Lawyer: T3]
- The people who share a portfolio can see each other's name, email address and phone number. [Lawyer: C7]
- No one else who uses the app can see where you live or your time zone: not the people in your portfolios, the caretaker included, and never tenants.
- Every member of a portfolio can see whether its owner lives abroad and who its caretaker is.
- A caretaker always uses the app, as a member with the manager role. Only an owner can name or change the caretaker. Someone stops being caretaker if they leave the portfolio or an owner removes them, if their role changes, or if their account is deleted.
- Tenants do not use the app. They see only what you give them: receipts and statements, which carry the portfolio's letterhead and your details as the issuer, and the WhatsApp messages you send.
- CNIC numbers are masked on screen by default; the full number shows only when you choose to see it.
- Identity documents and photos are held in private storage, and are opened only through links that expire after a short time. Letterhead files are held in private storage too, kept apart for each portfolio. An owner can copy a portfolio's letterhead to their other portfolios, which may have other members. [Owner: which roles may see and change a letterhead. Lawyer: L4]
- The people who run the app for us can reach the database to keep it working, to restore it after a fault and to answer your requests. [Owner: who they are, and the rule for when they may look at records.] [Lawyer: R5]

#### Why we use it

- To run the app for you and the people you invite: your account, your records, receipts, reminders and the audit trail.
- To keep the app and your account secure, and to restore your records after a mistake or an outage.
- To send you the emails the service needs, described under "Emails".
