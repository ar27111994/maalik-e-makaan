# Collect rent with Raast Request-to-Pay, through a licensed partner

Milestone **Money that moves** (`money`), due 30 November 2028. Issue `raast-request-to-pay` in
[`.github/roadmap.toml`](../../.github/roadmap.toml). The partner search and the legal opinion are
owner work that can start in 2027; no code beyond a sandbox spike comes before both.

Part of the [roadmap](README.md). "Decision 1", "decision 2" and "decision 3" below are the

[residence](README.md#decision-1-residence-per-person-and-per-portfolio-d-16),

[privacy-first analytics, support and e-mail](README.md#decision-3-privacy-first-analytics-support-and-e-mail-d-18).

## Why it matters

Today a tenant pays the way they always have, and the landlord or caretaker types the payment
in. A Raast request to pay puts the exact rent and the tenancy's reference into the tenant's own
bank app; one approval pays it, and the payment and its receipt appear in the ledger without
anyone typing them. An owner abroad sees rent arrive in their own account, not through a
caretaker's hands. The money goes through a licensed partner and never passes through AR LABS.

## What is true now

Checked on 11 November 2026. External facts marked _(summary)_ were read only through
search-engine summaries, because the primary pages could not be fetched.

- **Version 1 records payments and does not process them.** Collecting through JazzCash,
  Easypaisa or Raast APIs is out of scope ([REQUIREMENTS.md](../../.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md)),
  and the website says "Payments are recorded, not processed"
  ([features](../../website/src/content/features/en.yaml)). Changing that needs a decision entry;
  until then it is open proposal P-02 in [PROJECT.md](../../.planning/PROJECT.md).
- **The ledger already knows Raast**: the `raast` payment method
  ([ledger.sql](../../supabase/migrations/20261103193658_ledger.sql)) and the landlord's Raast ID
  in `portfolios.raast_id`
  ([identity migration](../../supabase/migrations/20261103192722_identity_portfolios.sql)).
- **`record_payment` needs a person.** It takes the lease lock only for an owner, manager or
  accountant, stores the caller as `received_by`, and is idempotent per portfolio and key
  ([ledger_findings.sql](../../supabase/migrations/20261107181820_ledger_findings.sql)). A
  partner's webhook has no person behind it. The name `payment_requests` is taken by the private
  table of idempotency fingerprints. The path is modelled in TLA+
  ([`LedgerPayments`](../../formal/tla/LedgerPayments)).
- **Request-to-Pay is mandatory for regulated entities.** Raast P2M launched in December 2023,
  and a circular of 5 December 2023 requires every bank, microfinance bank, EMI and PSP to
  process Request-to-Pay _(summary)_
  ([Profit](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2023/12/06/sbp-launches-raast-p2m-service-to-facilitate-digital-payment-acceptance)).
- **A route exists today.** PayFast payment links have triggered a Request-to-Pay in the payer's
  bank app since 2024
  ([PayFast](https://gopayfast.com/payfast-becomes-the-first-payment-gateway-to-enable-raast-p2m-payments-in-pakistan/)).
  One low-authority site reports "Raast Business API" licences for 1LINK, Finja, NayaPay,
  SadaPay Business and PayFast in August 2026, unconfirmed by the State Bank
  ([NextGen](https://nextgen.pk/news/sbp-raast-business-api-license-b2b-payments-pakistan)).
- **A licence of our own is out of reach.** The EMI Regulations list payment initiation and
  aggregation as EMI services, and an EMI needs Rs 200m of initial capital
  ([SBP EMI Regulations](https://www.sbp.org.pk/assets/document/C3-Enclosure-Regulations-EMIs.pdf)).
- **Fees.** The subsidy to banks on P2M QR payments (0.5%, at most Rs 100) ended on 30 June
  2026 _(summary)_
  ([SBP PSPOD Circular 3 of 2025](https://www.sbp.org.pk/circulars/pspod-circular-no-03-of-2025)).
  Merchant fees run from 0%
  ([Bank Alfalah](https://www.bankalfalah.com/business-banking/retail-payment-solution/raast-p2m-qr/))
  to 0.60% in an unofficial PayFast guide
  ([partner guide](https://www.scribd.com/document/890936512/Partner-Guide-PayFast)).
- **Person-to-person Raast is free** _(summary)_
  ([SBP DISD Circular 1 of 2022](https://www.sbp.org.pk/circulars/disd-circular-no-1-of-2022)),
  and QR codes are generated by financial institutions from the customer's IBAN _(summary)_
  ([SBP 2022 QR annex](https://www.sbp.org.pk/disd/2022/CL2-Annex.pdf)).

## Design

**A decision first.** A decision entry and a design record supersede "record, not process" for
this path only: AR LABS tells a licensed partner what to request and records what the partner
reports. Its threat model covers spoofed or replayed webhooks, fake requests in the landlord's
name, a changed payout account and a partner outage. Every other way of paying stays record-only.

**The partner model.** Each landlord becomes the partner's merchant or sub-merchant and passes
the partner's KYC in the partner's own flow. The partner issues each request and settles straight
to the landlord's own IBAN or wallet. No money passes through an AR LABS account; if it did, AR
LABS would be an aggregator. A legal opinion confirms that AR LABS, as a technical service
provider, needs no licence.

**Data.**

- `payment_partner_accounts`, one per portfolio: partner, merchant ID, status, the last four
  characters of the payout IBAN, who enabled it and when. Credentials live in Vault or Edge
  Function secrets, never in a table. Only owners create or change it.
- `raast_requests`, one per due: lease, charge, amount (`numeric(14,2)`), reference, the
  partner's request ID, expiry, status (`queued`, `sent`, `paid`, `expired`, `cancelled`,
  `failed`) and the resulting payment. A partial unique index allows one live request per due.
  Locks follow account, portfolio, lease, then the request.
- `raast_partner_events`: each webhook body with its verification result, unique by the
  partner's event ID, deleted after a set period.

**Functions.** A step in the existing ten-minute job queues requests for tenancies switched on;
`r2p-send` sends them, and cancels those whose due was paid another way or voided, or whose lease
ended. `r2p-webhook` checks the partner's signature and a timestamp window, stores the event and
records the payment. `r2p-poll` asks about requests still unpaid after a set time, so a lost
webhook only delays a payment.

**Recording without a person.** `record_payment` is split into the public function, which keeps
its role check, and a private core. The partner path calls the core with no person as actor:
method `raast`, the Raast transaction ID as reference, the request's due as `p_charge_ids`, and a
UUIDv5 key from the partner and its transaction ID, as [draft-payments](draft-payments.md)
derives keys from transfer references. It first looks for a non-voided payment on the lease with
that key or reference: if a person already entered the transfer, the request links to it. Both
paths take the lease lock. [audit-screen](audit-screen.md) attributes the write to `system`. A
different amount is allocated by the usual rules, and a payment after expiry is still recorded.
In TLA+, a partner that delivers webhooks late, twice and out of order while a person enters the
same transfer still makes each Raast transaction at most one payment.

**Fallback.** A tenant whose bank app does not show the request pays through the partner's link
or dynamic QR, or by free Raast to the landlord's ID. The app never draws a QR code itself.

### Residence (decision 1)

Only an owner connects a partner account, paying out to an account in the owner's name, never a
caretaker's. Whether an owner abroad can pass a partner's KYC (with a NICOP, a Pakistani account
or a Roshan Digital Account) is a question for each partner. A payout-account change raises the
high-severity finding of [overseas-oversight](overseas-oversight.md). Due dates stay Pakistan
dates; "rent received" notices follow the owner's own time zone and quiet hours.

### Letterheads (decision 2)

Receipts for partner payments carry the portfolio's letterhead. Letterheaded PDFs are rendered on
a device and a webhook has none, so the next owner or manager device that syncs renders the
receipt and stores it as the `receipt` document. The tenant's link shows the receipt number at
once and the PDF when it exists.

### Consent and analytics (decision 3)

The tenant's mobile number or Raast alias, the amount and the reference go to the partner. The
tenant switches requests on from their [tenant-portal](tenant-portal.md) link, recorded in
`tenant_consents` with the text version. The partner is named in the privacy notice. No amount,
name, reference or number reaches analytics; counts come from server aggregates.

## Risks

- **Treated as an unlicensed aggregator.** Mitigation: the legal opinion before any build;
  settlement only to the landlord, shown in the sandbox.
- **Partners refuse landlords without an NTN or business account.** Mitigation: ask before
  choosing; keep free Raast with [reconciliation](reconciliation.md) for the rest.
- **Fees now that the subsidy has ended.** Mitigation: a written rate card; fees always visible.
- **Phishing with fake requests.** Mitigation: the payee is the landlord's registered merchant
  name, and the tenant can check the live request on their link.
- **Duplicate or lost webhooks.** Mitigation: the derived key, the poll, the concurrency scenario
  and the TLA+ invariant.
- **Lock-in to one partner.** Mitigation: one adapter interface in the Edge Functions.

## Done when

- [ ] The decision entry is merged and the legal opinion is on file before the first migration.
- [ ] Each due has at most one live request, and ending a lease cancels its requests (pgTAP).
- [ ] Each partner payment is recorded exactly once with duplicate, late and out-of-order
      webhooks, a webhook caught only by the poll, and a manual entry of the same transfer.
- [ ] A forged, unsigned or replayed webhook records nothing (Deno tests).
- [ ] In the sandbox every settlement goes to the landlord's account, none to AR LABS.
- [ ] The landlord sees the fee before switching on and on each payment.
- [ ] No request goes to a tenant without their recorded consent.
- [ ] Every string exists in English and Urdu, and the design lint passes.
